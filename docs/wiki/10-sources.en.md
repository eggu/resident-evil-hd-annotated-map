Map data (coordinates, room mappings, pickup flags and appearance conditions) was derived from a legally owned copy of the game. No game executables, archives or saves are included. This is an unofficial fan mod, not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom.

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Evil Resource](https://www.evilresource.com/) | Cross-check of room names and contents; its text and images are not included |
| [RE3 Annotated Map](https://github.com/eggu/resident-evil-3-annotated-map) | Typewriter and item box icons |
| [Pretendard](https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard) | Map label and legend font (SIL OFL 1.1) |
| [Ultimate ASI Loader](https://github.com/ThirteenAG/Ultimate-ASI-Loader) | Bundled ASI loader v9.7.4 (MIT) |
| Zig (`ziglang`), Clang, Pillow | Build and test tools installed separately under their own licenses |

Icons are pixel art made for this mod with AI assistance. Item icons are style edits of the game's inventory images; enemy heads and badges were generated in the same style.
